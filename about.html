About Me

Originally from Chillicothe, Ohio, I graduated from Zane Trace High School in 2012. I obtained my Bachelor of Arts in Astrophysics with a minor in Religion from Ohio Wesleyan University in 2016. In 2022 I obtained my PhD in Astronomy from the University of Hawaiʻi Institute for Astronomy.

These days I love going hiking, bowling, playing softball, eating good food, and making good coffee.
