About Me

I’m an astronomical data scientist whose work combines stellar astrophysics, machine learning, and the tools researchers use to explore space-based survey data.

Science & Functional Work at STScI

My STScI work connects the science coming from space-based surveys with the archive systems researchers use to access it. On the functional side, I coordinate MAST operations for the Roman Space Telescope, contribute to data ingest, and guide products including the Light Curve Similarity Search and Hello Universe. On the science side, I develop methods for extracting stellar rotation and activity from large photometric datasets, using machine learning and light-curve analysis to study stellar ages and evolution. This builds on my Kepler and TESS research and supports the science potential of the upcoming Roman Galactic Bulge Time Domain Survey.
