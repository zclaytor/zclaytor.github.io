Zach's Blurbs

About Me

Originally from Chillicothe, Ohio, I earned a BA in Astrophysics from Ohio Wesleyan University and a PhD in Astronomy from the University of Hawaiʻi. I study how stars rotate, stay active, and change over time using telescope observations and computational tools.

Research Interests

Stars leave clues in their changing brightness. I use stellar light curves to measure rotation and magnetic activity, then use those measurements to learn how stars evolve and estimate their ages.
